I have been to Bagan twice, in 2018 and 2019. I explored the pagodas, watched the sunset and the hot air balloons, rode an e-bike around the area, and tried the local food.

Route

  1. 2018First visit
  2. 2019Second visit

Highlights

Visiting the pagodas and temples, and watching the sunset over Bagan.

Watching the hot air balloons in the sky.

Riding an e-bike from one place to another.

Trying Bagan's signature food. My favourite was toddy palm juice with fried snacks.

Tips

An e-bike is an easy way to get around the temple area.
